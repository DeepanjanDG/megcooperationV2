Accessibility Statement

How we make this website usable by everyone, the standards we follow, and how to tell us when something does not work for you.

This website is built so that it can be used by everyone, irrespective of technology, device or ability. We have made our best effort to ensure that all information published here is accessible to people with disabilities.

Our commitment

The website has been designed to provide the maximum possible accessibility and usability to its visitors. It can be viewed from a wide range of devices — desktop and laptop computers, tablets, web-enabled mobile phones and assistive devices.

Content on this website complies with the Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW) and conforms to the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA, to the extent feasible.

Accessibility features on this site

Text size control

Use the A-, A and A+ buttons in the bar at the top of every page to decrease, reset or increase the size of the text. Your choice is remembered as you move between pages.

High contrast mode

The High contrast button at the top of every page switches the site to a dark, high-contrast colour scheme for improved readability.

Skip to main content

Every page begins with a “Skip to Main Content” link so that keyboard and screen-reader users can bypass the navigation and go straight to the page content.

Keyboard navigation

All menus, links, form fields and buttons can be reached and operated using the Tab, Enter and arrow keys, without a mouse.

Structured, readable pages

Pages use proper headings, landmarks and lists, tables carry row and column headers, and the layout reflows cleanly on small screens.

Alternative text

Images that carry meaning are given descriptive alternative text. Decorative images are marked so that screen readers skip over them.

Content on external websites

Part of the information on this website is made available through links to external websites. Those websites are maintained by their respective owners, who are responsible for making their own sites accessible. The Department of Cooperation cannot guarantee the accessibility, availability or content of external websites.

Documents in other formats

Some documents on this site are published as PDF files, which open in a separate reader. If you are unable to read a document in the format in which it is published, please write to us and we will make the content available to you in an accessible alternative format.

Download Adobe Acrobat Reader (free) to view PDF documentsExternal

Tell us about a problem

We review this website regularly, but some pages — particularly older documents and scanned material — may not yet meet every accessibility requirement. If you find a page that you cannot use, please tell us what you were trying to do and which page you were on, and we will fix it or send you the information in another format.
