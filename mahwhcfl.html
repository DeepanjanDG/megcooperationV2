Apex Handloom Weavers Federation

The Meghalaya Apex Handloom Weavers Cooperative and Handicraft Federation Limited — supplying yarn to primary handloom societies and marketing their finished products.

Handloom occupies an important place in small-scale and village cottage industry in Meghalaya. The Federation supplies quality yarn to primary handloom societies and markets the finished products.

The Federation has been provided with share capital assistance of Rs. 3.00 lakh by the National Cooperative Development Corporation. It has paid-up share capital of Rs. 74.42 lakh, with a business turnover of Rs. 6.15 lakh and a gross profit of Rs. 1.25 lakh during 2003–2004. Its accumulated loss stands at Rs. 24.38 lakh.
