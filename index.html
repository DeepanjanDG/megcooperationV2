About the Department

Department of Cooperation,
Government of Meghalaya

Cooperatives are autonomous associations of persons united voluntarily to meet their common economic, social and cultural needs and aspirations through a jointly owned and democratically controlled enterprise.

The Department operates as an administrative department in the Secretariat, with the Office of the Registrar of Cooperative Societies as head of department administering societies across all eight districts.

Our Key Initiatives

Working for a Cooperative Meghalaya

Promotion

Encouraging the formation, registration and growth of cooperative societies across every sector of the state economy.

Know More  →

Capacity Building

Training, cooperative education and information through the Meghalaya State Cooperative Union and MCTI, Shillong.

Know More  →

Financial Support

Share capital contribution, managerial subsidy, working capital and godown loans to strengthen society balance sheets.

Market Linkage

Connecting cooperatives to markets through MECOFED and the apex marketing and processing societies.
