Functions and Duties

The legal framework the Department works within, the powers reserved to the State Government, and the roles of the Registrar and the officers below.

The Department functions in accordance with the Rules of Executive Business of the State of Meghalaya, framed under clauses 2 and 3 of Article 166 of the Constitution of India.

Its administrative roles, powers and functions are also guided by the Meghalaya Secretariat Manual of Office Procedure, the General Handbook of Circulars, the Fundamental Rules, the Subsidiary Rules 1984, the Delegation of Financial Power Rules 1981, and such other rules, regulations and subordinate legislation as are adapted or enacted from time to time.

The regulation and control of cooperative societies is exercised through the Acts and Rules. The Registrar of Cooperative Societies is friend, philosopher, regulator and guide, acting under the overall policy and other directions of the State Government.

Powers of the State Government under the Act

The following powers are reserved to the Government of Meghalaya under the Meghalaya Cooperative Societies Act as adapted, under Sections 44, 51, 118 and 120.

Section 44 — Financial assistance

Notwithstanding anything contained in any law for the time being in force, the Government of Meghalaya may grant loans to, take shares in, guarantee the principal or the interest or both in respect of debentures issued by, or give financial assistance in any other form to any registered society which puts forward a satisfactory scheme for the utilisation of the funds so raised.

The Government of Meghalaya may recover or recall from any society, out of its net profit in any year, all or any part of such financial assistance in the event of mis-utilisation (as amended by the Meghalaya Cooperative Societies Act, 2015).

Section 51 — Remission of tax, duty and fees

  1. The Government of Meghalaya may, by general or special order, in the case of a registered society or class of registered societies, remit any tax, cess or fee payable under any law for the time being in force or the rules thereunder, in respect of which it is competent to remit such tax, cess or fee.
  2. The Government of Meghalaya may, in respect of any registered society or class of registered societies, by notification in the Official Gazette, remit:
    • stamp duty other than stamp duties falling within item 91 or item 96 in List 1 of the Seventh Schedule to the Constitution of India, in respect of any instrument executed by, on behalf of, or in favour of a registered society, or by an officer or on behalf of a member of one, and relating to the business of such society — including any class of such instruments, cooperating demand certificates, or decisions, awards or orders of the Registrar or of arbitrators under the Act — in cases where, but for such remission, the society, officer or member would be liable to pay the stamp duty chargeable under any law for the time being in force; and
    • any fee payable by a registered society under any law for the time being in force for the registration of documents, or any court fee for the time being in force.

Section 118 — Exemption from provisions of the Act

The Government of Meghalaya may, by general or special order, exempt any registered society or class of registered societies from any of the provisions of the Act, or may direct that such provisions shall apply to such society with such modifications as may be specified in the order.

Section 120 — Exemption from registration requirements

Notwithstanding anything contained in the Act, the Government of Meghalaya may, by special order and subject to such conditions as it may impose, exempt any society from any of the requirements of the Act as to registration.

Powers and role of officers

Registrar of Cooperative Societies

Appointed by the Government of Meghalaya for the registration, supervision, assistance, counsel and control of registered societies — including arbitration, audit, liquidation and checking misappropriation — and for the overall development of the cooperative movement and control over all cooperative education, with the powers and responsibilities provided in the Cooperative Act and Rules of Meghalaya.

Assistant Registrar of Cooperative Societies

Delegated to look after the progress and development of cooperative societies across the whole district: registration, audit, supervision, amendment of bye-laws, arbitration and liquidation, together with all other work allotted by the Registrar of Cooperative Societies, Meghalaya.

Sub-Registrar of Cooperative Societies

Assists the Assistant Registrar of Cooperative Societies, supervising branches such as the Audit Branch, the Statistical Branch and the Arbitration Branch, and carrying out all work allotted by the Assistant Registrar.
