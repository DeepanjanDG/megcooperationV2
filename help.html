Help

Getting around this website, adjusting how pages look, and opening the documents we publish.

This page explains how to get around the website, how to change the way pages look, and what you need in order to open the documents published here.

Finding your way around

  • Main menu. The menu at the top of every page groups the site into About Us, Schemes & Programs, Cooperative Societies, Reports & Publications, RTI and Contact Us. Hover or click a heading to open its sub-menu.
  • Search. Use the search box in the header to jump straight to a page. Results appear as you type.
  • Breadcrumbs. The trail just below the header — for example Home / Help — shows where you are and lets you step back up.
  • Sitemap. The sitemap lists every page on this website on a single page.
  • On a phone or tablet. The menu collapses into a ☰ Menu button. Tap it to open the navigation.

Changing how pages look

Use the controls in the bar at the very top of every page to adjust the display. Your choice is remembered as you move from page to page.

A-   A   A+

Text size. A+ increases the text size, A- decreases it, and A returns the page to the default size.

High contrast

Colour scheme. Switches the site to a dark background with light text for stronger contrast. Press it again to go back to the standard scheme.

Skip to Main Content

Keyboard shortcut. The first link on every page. It takes you past the menu and straight into the page content.

Your browser and your device have their own accessibility settings as well — zoom, minimum font size, colour overrides and screen readers. See Accessibility Options in Browsers for step-by-step instructions.

Using the keyboard

  • Tab moves forward through links, buttons and form fields; Shift + Tab moves back.
  • Enter follows a link or presses a button. Space also presses a button and scrolls the page.
  • Escape closes an open menu or search result list.
  • Ctrl + + and Ctrl + - zoom the whole page in and out (Cmd on a Mac).

Opening documents and media

Some material on this site is published in formats that need a reader or player. All of the following are available free of charge.

File formats used on this website

Links to PDF documents and to external websites are labelled, and open in a new window, so that you know before you follow them. If you cannot open a document, write to us and we will send you the content in another format.
