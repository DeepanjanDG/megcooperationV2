Schemes for Development of Cooperative Societies

State plan, centrally sponsored and central sector schemes through which the Department supports cooperative societies in Meghalaya.

The Government provides financial assistance to cooperative societies so that they can strengthen their capital base, run their business, and improve the income and socio-economic condition of their members.

How a proposal is sanctioned

Proposals for sanctioning a scheme of the Department emanate from the cooperative societies themselves. They are examined by the concerned field officers and by a committee at the district level. Proposals are also initiated on directions and examined at the Directorate level.

Proposals received from the Directorate are subjected to further scrutiny in the Department and by the departmental committee at the state level. Based on guidelines from the Planning and Finance Departments and other departmental norms, financial sanctions are issued under the powers delegated, or are referred to the Planning and Finance Departments for approval and concurrence. The financial powers of officers are as per the Delegation of Financial Power Rules, 1981 and succeeding amendments issued by the Finance Department from time to time.

I. Under the State Plan

Financial assistance is provided in the shape of:

Share Capital Contribution

Government contribution to the share capital of a society, strengthening its capital base so that it can run its business and generate income for its members.

Managerial Subsidy

Assistance towards the cost of management, enabling societies to meet part of their establishment expenditure.

II. Centrally Sponsored Schemes (Government of India)

Financial assistance under centrally sponsored schemes covers:

  • Share Capital Contribution
  • Managerial Subsidy
  • Loans
  • Working Capital Loan
  • Godown Loan
  • Repair and renovation of godowns
  • Purchase of tools and implements

III. Central Sector Schemes through NCDC

Assistance to cooperative societies through the National Cooperative Development Corporation includes:

  • ICDP assistance for manpower development, training and business incentives
  • Assistance for construction of godowns and worksheds for primary handloom weavers
  • Share capital contribution to strengthen the share capital base of apex and primary marketing societies
  • Share capital for civil works, repair and renovation of godowns and worksheds
  • Share capital to apex and primary marketing societies as margin money
  • Loans for working capital, godown construction, and repairs and renovation

Objective of the schemes

Share capital contribution strengthens the share capital base of a society so that it can run its business and generate income, augmenting the income and socio-economic condition of poor tribal members.

Managerial subsidy enables societies to meet part of the expenditure on the cost of management.

Achievements and monitoring

Cooperative societies are welfare organisations in which individuals join together to augment their income-generating capacity through joint economic endeavour. The State Government extends financial assistance under various plan schemes to assist societies in achieving their objectives.

A system of periodic review of progress and of quality in scheme implementation is in place at both the state level and the district level.
