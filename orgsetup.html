Organisational Setup

How the Department of Cooperation is structured, from the Secretariat down to the sub-divisional offices.

The Department is organised at four levels — the Secretariat, the Directorate of the Registrar of Cooperative Societies, the eight district offices, and the sub-divisional offices.

Secretariat Level

The Department of Cooperation is one of the administrative departments under the Government of Meghalaya. As an administrative department it is responsible for policy decisions, sanctions and administrative directions in respect of the subjects allocated to it. The Department works under the overall guidance and direction of the Minister in charge of Cooperation, and is headed administratively by the Commissioner & Secretary.

Directorate Level — Registrar of Cooperative Societies

The Office of the Registrar of Cooperative Societies is a directorate under the Department. Other subordinate officers hold the ranks of Assistant Registrar (ARCS), Sub Registrar (SRCS) and Senior Cooperative Officer (SCO), with a Senior Superintendent and other assistants at the headquarters and in the zonal office at Tura.

District Level

In each of the eight districts — Jaintia Hills, East Khasi Hills, West Khasi Hills, South West Khasi Hills, Ri-Bhoi, East Garo Hills, West Garo Hills and South Garo Hills — the Department has an Office of the Assistant Registrar of Cooperative Societies, and one Sub-Registrar of Cooperative Societies in South West Khasi Hills District, Mawkyrwat. These offices have their own subordinate officers and staff.

Sub-Divisional Level

The Department also has offices of the Sub-Registrar of Cooperative Societies in two sub-divisions — Amlarem and Sohra — and one Sub-Registrar of Cooperative Societies in South West Khasi Hills District, Mawkyrwat, which is to be upgraded to the Office of the Assistant Registrar of Cooperative Societies.
