Meghalaya State Housing Financing Cooperative Society

The apex housing society, lending to affiliated primary housing cooperatives across the state.

The Meghalaya State Housing Financing Cooperative Society Limited is a registered cooperative society, registered on 10.05.1976 under the Meghalaya Cooperative Societies Act, 1950.

Objects

The main objects of the Apex Housing Society are to grant loans and advances to housing cooperative societies in Meghalaya that are affiliated to it — against the security of a mortgage of immovable property or such other security as may be considered necessary — for the construction of houses, repairs, additions or alterations and improvement, and to acquire and develop land for the benefit of housing programmes.

Its area of operation is the whole State of Meghalaya.

Membership

The Society has 31 members, including the State Government. The affiliated primary housing cooperative societies are distributed as follows.

Lending

The Apex Housing Society has 1,803 members in all. It has sanctioned Rs. 85.86 lakh as housing loans to 14 of its affiliated primary housing cooperative societies for the construction of houses, and 274 members of those societies have benefited through the construction of 274 houses for the less fortunate.
